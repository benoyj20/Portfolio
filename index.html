01Healthcare AI · 2026

SecondOpinion

1st place, Evidence Navigator · AI Healthcare Hackathon at Northeastern

Fact-checks cancer research reports by recomputing every statistic from the raw patient data. An LLM reads the language; deterministic code does all the math. Each claim is then recounted eight different ways to show whether its verdict holds or flips, and any overstated sentence is rewritten to what the data supports.

  • 100% on 214 claims
  • ~4 s per audit
  • 0 hallucinations
  • 4 role views

03Generative AI · RAG · 2026

TriageAI

2nd place · Google Developer Groups (GDG) Cloud Boston x Northeastern University Hackathon

IT support desks bury real outages under password resets. TriageAI reads every ticket in English or German, masks personal data before any model sees it, and decides who needs to act. Eleven safety rules send urgent incidents straight to on-call. For everything else, a RAG pipeline pulls the five closest solved cases from a Redis vector index, and an LLM drafts a reply that may only cite them, so nothing is made up.

  • 87% of urgent incidents caught
  • 0.89 macro F1 on 8,034 unseen tickets
  • 11 safety rules
  • 9 ms to classify

Python · FastAPI · Go · React · RAG · Redis vector search · OpenAI · Gemini · Docker

04Computer Vision · 2026

Kitchen Version Control

Version control for the physical world. Phone snapshots of a shared kitchen counter, taken from any angle, become a Git-style history of every object: what was added, moved or removed, and when. A fine-tuned YOLO detector spots each cup, plate, glass and piece of cutlery, tracking keeps every object's identity as the camera moves, and a Claude assistant answers questions like “where's the knife?” straight from the history.
